Computing digits of pi…

Working out the first digits of pi. Your result appears here in a moment.

The most digits anyone has computed is 314 trillion (StorageReview, 2025). A browser can’t hold that, so this page calculates up to the first 20 million digits itself, searches those, and estimates the rest.

Everything runs on your device. The number you type is never sent anywhere.

For a bigger lookup, the Pi-Search Page at angio.net searches a larger precomputed file.
